How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. If a tarp is fastened poorly, it can direct water where you do not want it or give out when the wind picks up. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.
