Moisture Monitoring

Commercial Water Damage Risk Assessment and Prevention

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. A West Jordan Restoration risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. We recommend annual moisture assessments for all commercial properties, with more frequent inspections for high-risk areas such as basements, mechanical rooms, and aging plumbing systems.
