Ceiling Water Damage Repair in West Jordan

When a brown circle finally appears above you, the leak has been at work for a while. Before any mark shows, water has traveled down a joist, saturated the insulation and collected on top of the drywall. Common sources are the roof, an air conditioning drain, a pipe running overhead, or the bathroom one story up. Our team finds where it starts before any patching is done.

Painting over the stain does not fix it. While the space overhead stays damp, the stain returns, and within 24 to 48 hours mold can begin on the drywall paper. We open only what we need to, pull out wet insulation, dry the joists and remaining drywall, then patch, texture and paint so the repair blends in. A sagging ceiling is a different matter. Stay out from under it and call.

What It Looks Like

  • Ceiling paint lifting off in blisters or peeling strips
  • Seams, tape lines and fastener heads becoming visible across the ceiling
  • Stains or streaks running down the wall just below the ceiling line
  • Hairline cracks spreading outward from a stained or damp spot
  • Patches of spotty discoloration that look like mold on the ceiling
  • Drywall that sags, bows or feels soft when pressed gently
Save or Replace

What Can Be Saved

It depends on the material, how long it stayed wet and how clean the water was. We test before we decide, and we tell you why.

What You Cannot See

The Damage You Do Not See

Sudden Collapse

Wet drywall is far heavier than dry and loses its grip on the fasteners. A bulge can drop a whole section onto whatever is underneath.

Asbestos in Older Ceilings

Asbestos was used in certain older plasters and popcorn finishes. Testing comes first in such a home, ahead of any cutting, scraping or demolition of the damaged section.

The Leak Is Somewhere Else

The wet spot is often not below the leak, since water rides pipes, ducts and joists before it falls. We check beyond the visible stain so soaked sections down the line are not overlooked.

Growth on the Hidden Side

Mold usually grows out of sight on the upper face of the panel and in the insulation above, both of which hold moisture well after the painted side dries.

How We Fix It

What Happens After You Call

1

Trace the Leak

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team follows the water to its origin and inspects the attic or room overhead. The leak has to be stopped before repairs make sense.

2

Make It Safe

We cut power to any wet fixture and move or cover the furniture underneath. Where water is trapped overhead, it gets drained under control into containers.

3

Open and Dry

Wet insulation is removed along with any drywall that cannot stay. The joists and nearby ceiling are dried with air movers plus a dehumidifier, and antimicrobial goes on wherever the water was dirty.

4

Repair and Finish

When everything reads dry, our crew replaces the insulation, hangs drywall, finishes the seams, blends the texture with the rest of the ceiling, then applies stain blocker and paint.

The Clock

The Damage Timeline

The first few hours

Hidden Travel Overhead

Water runs along joists and soaks into insulation. The drywall absorbs it from the back, so the room below may still look fine.

24 to 48 hours

Marks Start Showing

A stain forms and paint starts to blister. The paper layer becomes a place mold is able to grow, and heavy wet insulation bears down from above.

Days 3 through 7

Sagging Sets In

The panel turns soft and starts slipping off its fasteners. Between the joists the surface dips, joints split apart and a musty smell arrives below.

After a week

Sections May Fall

Saturated pieces may drop and carry soaked insulation down too. Mold is established in the cavity and the repair now covers a much larger area.
