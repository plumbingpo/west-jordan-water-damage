Storm Preparation for Homeowners in West Jordan, UT

Home / Blog / Preparing Your Home for Storms in West Jordan, UT

Storms can cause devastating water damage to homes and businesses in West Jordan, UT. Proper preparation before storm season and before individual storm events can significantly reduce the damage your property sustains and the cost of restoration afterward. This checklist from West Jordan Restoration covers everything West Jordan homeowners should do to prepare.

Seasonal Preparation

Ahead of the season, do the following: check the roof for flashing or shingles that are missing, damaged, or worn out. Clear out every gutter and downspout, then confirm water discharges the way it should. Look over every door, window, and exterior penetration, and seal around each one. Inspect your sump pump and test the backup power system. Go over your insurance policy, including what it says about wind and flood damage. Document your property with current photos for insurance records. Branches driven by wind cause less harm when nearby trees have been trimmed.

Before a Storm Event

Once a storm is forecast, tie down loose outdoor items before they can turn into projectiles. Shut the storm shutters, or put boards over windows, when the forecast warrants it. Ensure your sump pump is operational and the battery backup is charged. Important papers and valuable items belong upstairs or inside containers that are waterproof. Know your evacuation route and have supplies ready. Program West Jordan Restoration's emergency number ((424) 507-2151) into your phone.

After the Storm Ends

Once it has passed, begin by inspecting the property from somewhere safe, and do not go in until you have. Document all damage with photos and video. Holes in the roof should be covered with a tarp, but only when that is safe. Electrical hazards must be assessed before anyone steps into a flooded area. Report the damage to your insurance company.
