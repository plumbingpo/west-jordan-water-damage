Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, West Jordan Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout West Jordan to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
