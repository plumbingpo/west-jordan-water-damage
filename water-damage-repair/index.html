How Our Water Damage Repair Works

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

Final moisture readings are taken on the subfloor, framing and any drywall left in place. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • Grout is breaking up and some tiles have lifted where moisture reached the floor below.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Damage Repair?

This is not the same as a standard renovation project. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. Our team works from the moisture records taken during drying, so we know which studs were wet, which subfloor was treated and where to look twice. Knowing the background stops issues from being hidden behind new wallboard and a coat of paint.

The second skill is matching. If the patch stands out, it reminds you of the damage daily. We blend wall texture, feather paint to natural break points and source flooring that lines up with the existing boards where it can be found. Should a match prove impossible, we present choices like replacing the floor up to a doorway. Permits, where required for the work, are handled by us, and your insurer stays informed.

Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
