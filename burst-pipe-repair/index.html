A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, West Jordan Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across West Jordan and the nearby communities. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in West Jordan

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
