Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. West Jordan Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout West Jordan using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Why Professional Water Extraction Matters

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Program (424) 507-2151 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency

Signs You Need Water Extraction in West Jordan

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
